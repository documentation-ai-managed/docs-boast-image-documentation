    <CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,Python">
      ````javascript
const feedback = await boast.collect({
  projectId: 'proj_123',
  imageUrl: 'https://example.com/design.png',
  reviewers: ['alice@company.com', 'bob@company.com']
});
console.log('Feedback collected:', feedback);
````
      ````python
import boast

feedback = boast.collect(
    project_id='proj_123',
    image_url='https://example.com/design.png',
    reviewers=['alice@company.com', 'bob@company.com']
)
print(f'Feedback collected: {feedback}')
````

## Quick Setup

Getting started with Boast takes just a few minutes. Create your account, upload your first project, and begin collecting valuable feedback immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit boast.com and create your free account using your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Project" icon="folder-plus">
    Set up your first project and define your team members and reviewers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Content" icon="image-plus">
    Upload your designs, screenshots, or website captures to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share & Collect" icon="send">
    Generate sharing links and begin gathering feedback from your stakeholders.
  </Step>
</Steps>
